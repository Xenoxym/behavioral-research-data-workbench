Skip logic and merge checks

Interpretation and limits

  • All counts, percentages and quantiles describe the analyzed public-use sample only. Survey weights, strata and PSUs are not applied; these are not US national estimates.
  • The case is cross-sectional, not a longitudinal study or randomized trial; it cannot estimate intervention effectiveness, causation, quit rates or follow-up completion.
  • The paired sample excludes adults without a classifiable cigarette response or an available serum cotinine result; missing biospecimens may introduce selection bias.
  • Fewer than 100 lifetime cigarettes is a questionnaire category, not proof of no current cigarette use or no nicotine exposure.
  • Cotinine reflects nicotine exposure from multiple sources, including cigarettes, secondhand exposure, nicotine replacement therapy and electronic nicotine products. Those sources and timing are not resolved by these three files.
  • No cotinine cutoff, clinical diagnosis, abstinence verification or honesty label is assigned. Differences between self-report and a biomarker require contextual research.
  • Below-detection results retain the CDC-supplied LLOD/sqrt(2) fill values (published rounded values) and detection flags. Quantiles near the detection limit are censored descriptions, not precise concentrations.
  • Age is age at screening and is top-coded at 80 years. The files combine 2017-2018 and the incomplete 2019-March 2020 cycle; they must not be split using these weights.
